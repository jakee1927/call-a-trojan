# AI@USC hackathon: rules and rubric (from ai-usc.devpost.com, read Nov 14 2026)

- In-person, two days: Sat Nov 14 – Sun Nov 15, 2026, Ginsburg Hall, USC. Hosted by HackSC x USC AI Committee with USC Student Government (USG & GSG).
- Theme: AI-powered tools for everyday USC pain points: **how student organizations recruit members, run events and share resources, and how students find the information, services and opportunities they need.**
- "USC Student Government intends to take the strongest solutions and work to implement them."
- **Deadline: Sunday Nov 15, 11:00 AM PT** on Devpost. Late or incomplete submissions are not judged.
- Required in the submission: a project description, a **working prototype link**, a **demo video (≤ 3 min)**, a **slide deck**, at least one image.
- Originality: all code, design assets and prototypes must be made during Nov 14–15. Open-source libraries, frameworks and public APIs are allowed.
- Teams of 1–4. Open only to current USC students.
- Judging: round 1 (11 AM – 2 PM Sun) judges review all submissions and pick 10 finalists. Finalists pitch live from 3:00 to 4:30 PM, up to 3 minutes each.
- Panel: faculty, industry leaders and student leaders, including USG representatives.

## Weighted scoring
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Impact & Campus Relevance | 30% |
| Technical & AI Execution | 25% |
| Feasibility & Implementation | 25% |
| User Experience & Design | 20% |

## Prizes
$14k cash across the top three teams, plus hardware, a guaranteed interview with K1 Investments, and a referral of top projects to USG for implementation.
